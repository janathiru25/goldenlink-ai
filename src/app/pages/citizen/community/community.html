HOW GOLDENLINK WORKS

One community. Different roles.

GoldenLink helps nearby people contribute according to their availability and verified capabilities.

01

Report

A citizen reports an accident and shares the essential location information.

02

Match

Suitable nearby responders can be identified based on their availability and profile.

03

Coordinate

Different people can receive different support roles instead of duplicating the same task.

04

Handover

Community response transitions when appropriate professional responders arrive.

Every trained person can become part of the response network.

GoldenLink is designed to coordinate people around an incident, not replace professional emergency services.
